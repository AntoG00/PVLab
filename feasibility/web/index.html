    Conflicts retained

    • Local research reports no CER; an earlier POD-based GSE map lookup returned configurations. CER revenue is disabled. Availability remains disputed.
    • The local cost formula and package benchmark differ. Default costs are calibrated to the package benchmark; the original formula is a sensitivity.
    • The supplied capture price and baseload × capture factor differ. Both are modeled without applying capture twice.
    • The dormer annual yield and shade-mask result conflict. Dormer and flat-rack layouts remain experimental.

    CORE DEFINITIONS

    Key financial and energy metric definitions

    Authoritative definitions of primary metrics across the lab, anchored to discounted cash-flow conventions and physical dispatch simulation.

    Discounted Net Present Value (NPV)

    The net economic surplus of the investment over the configured project horizon in present purchasing power, discounted at the nominal hurdle rate (or equivalent real rate). Compares the solar/package system directly against remaining on grid supply. Invariant across real and nominal display modes.

    Discounted Payback Period

    The earliest operational year in which cumulative discounted net cash flows permanently exceed the initial turnkey capex and loan financing expenses, verified across individual weather analogue paths.

    Autarky (Household Demand Coverage)

    The fraction of annual household electricity consumption supplied directly by solar PV or discharged from the paired battery (1 − Grid Imports / Household Demand). Measures self-sufficiency.

    Self-Consumption Rate

    The fraction of total annual solar PV generation consumed on-site—either immediately by domestic loads or routed to charge the battery (1 − Grid Exports / Total PV Generation). Measures solar utilization.

    Year-1 Net Cash Flow

    Net pocket cash benefit in the first operational year, consisting of avoided electric bills and net export earnings, plus the first annual IRPEF tax deduction installment, minus loan debt service (principal and interest). Deflated to installation purchasing power when viewed in real euros.

    Levelized Cost of Electricity (LCOE)

    Total lifetime lifecycle costs (turnkey capex, inverter and battery replacements, plus routine O&M, discounted over the configured project horizon) divided by total lifetime AC electricity generation. Signed LCOE advantage represents the direct discount per kWh relative to grid retail tariffs.
