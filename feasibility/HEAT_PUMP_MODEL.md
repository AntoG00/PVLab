# Heat-pump model

Monthly carrier consumption is allocated to explicit end uses, then converted
to useful thermal energy using the entered heating value and seasonal equipment
efficiency. Useful heat is distinct from compressor electricity. Cooking remains
separate from domestic hot water and space heating.

Temperature-weighted allocation preserves monthly energy budgets. Operating
windows use explicit civil or fixed-standard clock conventions, including
wrapping windows and DST. Monthly gas data cannot identify building thermal
mass, dynamic heat loss or a recovery spike after an off period.

The shared outdoor-unit model can include capacity limits, performance maps,
weather compensation, domestic-hot-water storage, auxiliaries and backup heat.
A provisional performance map is an illustrative fallback that must be disclosed;
it is not a manufacturer rating or a measured seasonal efficiency. Equipment
replacement requires separated baseline electrical evidence. Already-electric
uses cannot simply be added again to gross meter demand.

Public fixtures are analytic and fictional. They exercise allocation, schedules,
calendar handling, tank/controller behavior, cache identity and energy accounting.
They make no claim to reproduce a real dwelling. Historical household inputs and
frozen compatibility outputs remain external to the exportable tree.
